## 4. The decisive empirical object — FX-variance-share sensitivity surface

This section is the iteration's load-bearing anti-fishing safeguard. A reviewer must read it before §8.

### 4.1 Why a naive "β > 0" is trivially true and is NOT a finding

The analyst's local-currency cost stream is `cost = Q × c × FX`, where `c = $0.01`. **If Q were fixed**, `cost` is a deterministic scalar multiple of FX and the realized variance of the cost stream is mechanically proportional to the realized variance of FX — a regression of cost-variance on FX-variance returns β > 0 by pure algebra. That is not a finding.

### 4.2 The exact variance decomposition (no leading-order caveat; daily gapped grid — amended CORRECTIONS-E10-7)

In E10, **Q is stochastic** — a simulated workflow process (§6). Y, X, and the decomposition are defined in **log-return space**. Because `c` is constant, `Δlog cost = Δlog Q + Δlog FX` is an *exact identity*, and the variance of a sum is exactly the sum of variances plus twice the covariance:

> Var(Δlog cost) = Var(Δlog FX) + Var(Δlog Q) + 2·Cov(Δlog FX, Δlog Q).

This is **exact**, with no higher-order term and no approximation. The level-variance object `Var(cost)` has no clean additive split and is **banned** from the §4.4 share (§5.3).

**CORRECTIONS-E10-7 amendment — the decomposition grid.** v0.6 §4.2 stated the identity for `Var(·)` on a *daily grid* without anticipating the zero-Q domain failure. Because `Δlog(cost)` is undefined on a zero-Q day (`cost = 0` there), the three-way decomposition is computed on the **daily grid restricted to non-zero-Q days** — the common surviving index. Per cell, the zero-Q days are dropped from X and Q on the SAME surviving index before differencing; the identity then holds **exactly per cell** because `log(cost_b) − log(cost_a) = (log Q_b − log Q_a) + (log FX_b − log FX_a)` is exact for any pair of days on which Q, FX, and cost are all positive. The Phase-3 (E10.2) implementation verified this empirically: the maximum `|identity_residual|` across all 150 panel cells is ~1.3e-15 (floating-point zero). A cell with fewer than two surviving positive-Q days carries `material_gap = True` and routes to the §9 ladder; all 150 panel cells retain ≥ 2 surviving days, so the decomposition computes panel-wide. The ~51% zero-Q-day prevalence and the log-domain-forced nature of the drop are documented in §0.7.

### 4.3 The FX-variance share is a calibration-conditional sensitivity surface (CLOSED in v0.3)

The FX-variance share is

> FX-variance share = Var(Δlog FX) ÷ Var(Δlog cost),

both terms computed on the common daily gapped grid of §4.2. `Var(Δlog FX)` is real (central-bank data). `Var(Δlog Q)` and the covariance are simulator outputs. The honest treatment: the FX-variance share is **NOT a single number** — it is a **calibration-conditional sensitivity SURFACE** across the defended (anchored) Q-volume range of §6.2. **The surface is the primary deliverable of E10 v0.7** (§9 SURFACE-PRODUCED).

### 4.4 The material threshold — the fitted hedge's break-even, pinned EX ANTE (CORRECTIONS-E10-4 Fix 2 — carried forward)

**Unchanged from v0.6 / v0.5 / v0.4.** The material threshold is the **fitted hedge's break-even share** — the FX-variance share at which the convex payoff's expected return covers its stream-funded premium. It is pinned **ex ante** at `s_be = 0.25` (the Phase-2.5 break-even record, `notebooks/e10_gsps/diagnostics/E10.2.5_break_even_threshold.md`), derived from option-premium economics against an ex-ante-pinned strike/range geometry and premium fraction (§13.2) — independent of the §4 decomposition.
