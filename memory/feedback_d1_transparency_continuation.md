D1 (Colombian USD-paid remote workers) stays open as Direction 1.D (on-chain crypto rails) under a standing continuation condition: **at every iteration step, transparently disclose what data is needed, what data quality is actually achieved, and what the expected outcomes are — including the possibility that the iteration may not retire / close the cohort question and the possibility that data quality won't support firm conclusions.**

**Why:** The just-completed D1 G1 gating step found the cohort exists empirically (75K central, 25-150K range, 25-55% YoY) but isn't measurable at monthly frequency on standard public data (GEIH FAIL empirically; Banrep CoE 92× too small; ADP descoped). The salvage path (on-chain crypto rails via Bitso/Lemon hot wallets) is viable but has two binding gaps: Colombian-resident attribution (20% scalar with 10-35% sensitivity) and contractor-vs-speculator attribution (Layer C, deferred to Phase 2). The full cohort coverage is 15-35%, not 100%. The user's continuation condition (2026-05-18) is that D1.D may proceed *if and only if* these limitations are explicitly stated at every step rather than buried.

**How to apply:**
- Every D1.D notebook trio must include a "data-quality disclosure" interpretation block alongside the standard decision-citation block
- Every D1.D plan / spec must explicitly enumerate (a) what data is required, (b) what data is actually available, (c) what gaps remain, (d) what outcomes are possible *including* the negative outcome that the iteration cannot retire the question
- Phase-1 results must explicitly state the 15-35% coverage caveat and what the gating-step verdict would mean under each coverage assumption
- HALT-on-spec-vs-data invariants carry forward unchanged, but additional anti-fishing posture: "we may not be able to retire this iteration" is an acceptable interim conclusion that does NOT trigger threshold-tuning
- This is NOT a license to drag out the iteration indefinitely; it is a license to honestly close it at FAIL or PARTIAL-PASS without escalation pressure to manufacture a clean verdict
