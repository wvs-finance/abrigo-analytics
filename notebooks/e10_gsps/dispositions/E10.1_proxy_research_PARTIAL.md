### 2.1 `Q_low` — the §2.4 qualitative-limit threshold — UNPINNED

The proxy research reached The Graph's published pricing/billing docs and
the project's `x402_subgraph_design_space.md`. The decisive finding:

- The free tier's qualitative limit is a **capability threshold, not a
  query-volume threshold.** The free tier becomes inadequate when the
  workload requires `trace_*`/`debug_*` archive methods, large-range
  (`>10k`-block) `eth_getLogs`, cross-protocol curated SQL, or a protocol
  with no maintained subgraph (`x402_subgraph_design_space.md` Q1/Q2,
  lines 95-218). None of these is a function of *how many* queries the
  analyst runs.
- The Graph publishes **no per-second / per-minute rate limit** for the
  free plan — only the 100k/mo volume cap (confirmed by direct fetch of
  both the Studio pricing page and the billing docs;
  `x402_subgraph_design_space.md` open item 6 already flagged this).
- Combined with the calibration-note §5 finding — the metadata-only
  observed trace structurally **cannot observe** whether a given query
  *needed* an archive/trace capability (it stores only a coarse host/op)
  — there is no observable and no published proxy that maps a *volume*
  to the qualitative limit.

**Conclusion:** `Q_low` **cannot be pinned as a scalar query-volume
number.** The §2.4 rule asks for "the volume at which the user's own
observed query logs first exhibit a qualitative pattern the free tier
cannot serve" — but the proxy research establishes that the qualitative
limit is *not volume-indexed*; it binds on workload *capability*, which
is both unobservable in the trace and not a universal scalar. `Q_low`
is recorded as **UNPINNED** — the engine carries it as `float("nan")`
(`calibrated_intensity_parameters(q_low=...)` default), a visible
"unpinned" marker, NOT a fabricated default.

### 2.2 Month-end-seasonality magnitude (modulation 4) — UNPINNED

No public The Graph query-volume statistic, RPC-provider telemetry, or
Dune usage statistic discloses a month-end reporting-cadence *magnitude*.
Dune publishes refresh-cadence facts only (popular dashboards hourly,
others daily) — not month-end volume seasonality. The calibration note
§4.4 already established the own-trace is too short (~11 days, one month)
to isolate a reporting bump from sprint clustering. **The magnitude
remains UNPINNED.** The engine carries the modulation-4 slot with
`month_end_bump_magnitude` defaulting to **0.0** — a documented no-op
bump, NOT a flat-fill: the *shape* is wired and pre-committed; only the
*magnitude* is unset, exactly the calibration-note §4.4 form/magnitude
split.

## 3. Why this is PARTIAL, not HALT, not flat-fill

- **Not HALT.** Both items were flagged **non-HALT and spec-anticipated**
  by the calibration note (§7), the RC pre-Phase-2 review (§6), and the
  Model QA pre-Phase-2 review (WEAK-1). Spec §6.2 explicitly routes
  bracket magnitudes to proxy research; §2.4 explicitly defers `Q_low`
  to a proxy-corroborated rule. The simulator *build* (task 2.1) and the
  calibrated *centre* (task 2.2 mechanical sub-step) are complete and
  unaffected — the engine builds the slots; only two bracket magnitudes
  are unfilled.
- **Not flat-fill, not fabricated.** Per CORR-E10P-8: the bracket is not
  invented. `Q_low` = NaN (unpinned marker); month-end magnitude = 0.0
  (documented no-op). Neither is an "arbitrary substitute bracket."
- **PARTIAL consequence (spec §9 / CORR-E10P-8).** Without the bracket
  magnitudes the §4.3 FX-variance-share surface degrades from a *band*
  to a *point/centre-only* characterization on the dimensions those two
  items would have widened. The Phase-6 §9 verdict must carry this
  forward: the surface is the calibrated-centre surface; the month-end
  and `Q_low` bracket dimensions are documented gaps.
