## 5. Descriptive verdict (spec §9 ladder — collectively exhaustive)

**NON-RETIREMENT.**

Spec §9 NON-RETIREMENT condition: "**The DV gate fails (a required free data
source is unreachable).**" Three required free data sources (SARB daily ZAR,
CBK daily KES, BoG daily GHS) are unreachable / not machine-readable for the
pinned panel window. The DV gate, re-verified per-currency at E10.0 as the spec
§0 record instructed, fails.

This is **not PARTIAL**: PARTIAL is "the surface is computed but with material
gaps — e.g. *a* panel currency drops." Three of eight currencies dropping —
including with no surface yet computed — is a DV-gate failure upstream of any
surface, which §9 routes to NON-RETIREMENT, not PARTIAL. The §7 field-7c HALT
("the surface cannot be computed across the anchored range … DV-gate fail") is
the same routing.

The spec §8 anti-fishing invariants forbid the escape hatches: **no
panel-currency expansion** (the user chose demote-not-expand on 2026-05-20 — a
substitute EM currency cannot be swapped in for ZAR/KES/GHS), **no panel-window
extension** to manufacture cells, and a paid data source is never justified
inside E10 (`feedback_dune_last_resort_exhaust_free_resources.md`). A
5-currency panel (G≈5 clusters) is materially below the 8-currency design and
the iteration's own descriptive posture; running E10.1+ on it would be a silent
re-spec, which is BANNED.

**Methods-paper §5 hook preserved.** Per spec §12, on NON-RETIREMENT the
qualitative GSPS conceptual contribution — the convex / cost-side
generalization of the streamed-liability primitive, and the
FX-variance-share-as-sensitivity-surface *construction* — stands as a
methods-paper §5 artifact in its own right. The x402 cost-multiplier
verification ($0.01 USDC flat, re-confirmed) and the regime-break screen
methodology are reusable. The empirical panel is parked.
