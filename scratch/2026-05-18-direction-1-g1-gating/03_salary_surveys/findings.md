## Recommendation

**CONDITIONAL PASS for the salary-survey sub-leg.**

1. **Wage-level anchors**: PASS — 3 independent USD-cohort sources converge tightly
2. **Cohort-size anchor**: CONDITIONAL — order-of-magnitude only; D1.2 (Banrep BoP) + D1.4 (Fermi triangulation) cross-validation required
3. **ADPRI**: UNAVAILABLE — confirmed null result
4. **Time series 2020-2026**: INCOMPLETE — only growth rates; no clean panel; β-estimate sampling must inherit DANE GEIH's panel structure (or pivot to Banrep BoP CoE per D1.2)

**Key spec-relevant finding**: the cohort is structurally **CONTRACTOR-classified**, not employee-classified, due to Colombian labor law (employee payroll MUST be in COP). This explains the GEIH null result (D1.1) — the instrument covers employer-employee relationships and "cuenta propia" but doesn't separate domestic-client contractors from foreign-client (USD-paid) contractors. Spec v0.3 amendment should reframe cohort as "contractor-class natural-person service exporters paid in foreign currency."
